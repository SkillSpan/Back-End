## Key conventions learned
- `career_roles.status` enum is `draft|approved|retired` (not `pending`).
- `scale` items intentionally skip allowed-option validation (options are scale anchors; DS accepts raw self-rated value).
- Answer validation is **fail-closed**: if the live item row is missing/options empty, single_choice answers are rejected rather than accepted. This deliberately preserves validation even after a live bank edit (needed to keep the immutability test meaningful).
- DS `baseline` endpoint additions are additive/nullable — an unchanged FastAPI service still works. Required DS change documented in `DYNAMIC_BASELINE_ASSESSMENT.md`.
- Readiness `ASSESSMENT_INCOMPLETE` completeness check was left untouched.
